Tailpiece ASR

Upscale lossy audio on your Mac. Support and help.

Need help or found a bug?

Open an issue on GitHub. Say which Mac and macOS version you're using, which engine you chose, and what kind of file it was (MP3, AAC, etc.).

Getting started

  • Drag an audio file onto the window, or press ⌘O.
  • Choose an engine: Harmonic Band Extension (DSP) is instant; AudioSR (diffusion) generates new high-frequency detail and takes longer.
  • Press ⌘R to upscale, then compare Before and After with ⌘1 / ⌘2 and Space to play.
  • Save the result with ⇧⌘S as WAV, FLAC or Apple Lossless.

Batches

Drop several files or a folder, click Choose Folder… to pick where results go, then Upscale All. Files render one after another and nothing is ever overwritten. Select a queued file and press ⌘⌫ to remove it before it starts.

Common questions

Why is AudioSR slow? It's a diffusion model running entirely on your Mac. Expect roughly 4 seconds of work per second of audio at 50 steps on an M2, and more at higher step counts. The first render also loads the model, which takes about 40 seconds. DSP renders in a fraction of a second.

Does it recover the original audio? No. Nothing can recover what the encoder threw away. AudioSR generates a plausible replacement that fits the music, and DSP extends what's already there. The spectrogram and the A/B player let you judge the result for yourself.

Requirements: a Mac with Apple silicon, running macOS 14 or later.
