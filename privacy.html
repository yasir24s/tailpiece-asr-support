Privacy Policy

Tailpiece ASR for Mac · Last updated 24 September 2026

Tailpiece ASR does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

All audio processing happens on your Mac. The app makes no network connections, contains no analytics or advertising, and does not track you. Files you open are read only to process them, and results are saved only where you choose.

In detail

  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for or sign in to.
  • No network access. The upscaling models ship inside the app and run offline. The app never uploads your audio or anything about it.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or advertising. No third-party SDKs of any kind are included.
  • Files. The app is sandboxed by macOS and can only open files and folders you choose. If you pick a folder for batch results, the app remembers that choice on your Mac so it can save there next time. That setting never leaves your Mac.
  • Preferences, such as theme and engine settings, are stored locally by macOS.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
